About us

We have been working with crystals for 22 years. Our pieces are made at our own factory in Anfeng, Donghai, China's crystal capital, and every piece is cleansed in the spring water of our courtyard before it ships.

We describe each stone as it is: where it comes from, its size, and whether it is raw or polished. Crystal traditions mentioned on this page are cultural practices, not health or medical claims.
